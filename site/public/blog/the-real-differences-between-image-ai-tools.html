Frequently Asked Questions (Extended)

Is it better to use one tool or multiple tools for image generation in 2026?

Many teams use a mix to cover realism and style needs. A single tool may not meet all prompts, so a small toolkit often yields the best balance of quality and speed.

How do I verify that AI-generated images are fit for brand use?

Check licensing, export rights, and required attributions. Also ensure the style aligns with brand guidelines and that you can reuse assets across channels.

What should I look for in an ai tool's privacy and data handling policy?

Look for how prompts and outputs are stored, whether your prompts are used to train models, and what data rights you retain.

Do image AI tools integrate with existing asset management systems?

Many tools offer plugins or export options for asset managers and design systems. If you rely on a PIM or DAM, verify compatibility before buying.

What is the best path to starting with image AI in a small team?

Start with free tiers on a couple of tools to confirm fit, then pick one or two that map to your most common prompts and licensing needs. This minimizes risk and speeds up adoption.
