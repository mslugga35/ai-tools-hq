Transcription quality in 2026: how to evaluate accuracy beyond marketing claims

Distributed teams run meetings with different accents, audio setups, and speaker overlap. That’s why transcription quality matters more than most AI features.

When teams compare Best AI Meeting Assistants for Distributed Teams 2026, they should understand what “accuracy” really means. A common framing is Word Error Rate (WER), where 95% accuracy implies ~5% WER (about 1 in 20 words wrong).

So instead of asking “Is it 90% accurate?”, ask “Does it preserve the details my team needs?” For example, names, decisions, deadlines, product terms, and technical requirements.

  • Reality check: even strong transcription can still require lightweight human review for critical entities.
  • Distributed-team focus: test multi-speaker overlap and meetings with background noise.
  • Action items output: verify that the summary doesn’t miss owners, commitments, or next steps.

Practical tip: Run one 30-minute call through any candidate tool and then check the “action items” section. If owners and deadlines are incomplete, the transcript may be “good enough” but the workflow is not.
