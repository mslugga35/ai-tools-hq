## Pre-Production: Research & Scripting

### 1. Claude — Best for Research, Outlines, and Content Strategy

**Price:** Free / $20/mo (Pro) / $100/mo (Max)
**Free Tier:** Yes

Claude is the best AI for the thinking phase of YouTube production — the part where you go from vague idea to structured video outline with researched talking points.

Give Claude your niche, your last 10 video topics, and your analytics (which videos performed best), and it will identify content gaps and suggest topics with high potential. It can analyze competitor channels, spot trending subtopics, and build complete video outlines with hooks, key points, and calls to action.

Where Claude dominates is research depth. If you're making a video about "best budget cameras for YouTube in 2026," Claude will compile specs, price points, pros and cons, and comparison tables — work that would take you two hours of Googling. Its 200K context window means you can paste entire product reviews, forum threads, and spec sheets, and it synthesizes everything into talking points.

We use Claude at the top of every production pipeline. The outline it produces becomes the blueprint for the script, which we then write (or co-write with ChatGPT) in the next step.

**Pros:**
- Best analytical thinking for content research and strategy
- 200K context handles massive research inputs
- Excellent at identifying content gaps in your niche
- Produces structured outlines that map directly to video chapters

**Cons:**
- No web browsing (use ChatGPT for real-time research, Claude for analysis)
- Free tier throttles during peak hours
- Slightly slower than ChatGPT for quick brainstorming

### 2. ChatGPT — Best for Script Writing and Hooks

**Price:** Free / $20/mo (Plus) / $200/mo (Pro)
**Free Tier:** Yes

ChatGPT takes Claude's outline and turns it into a watchable script. Its conversational style produces scripts that sound natural when read aloud — a critical quality most AI writing tools miss. YouTube scripts that read like blog posts kill watch time.

The web browsing feature is invaluable for keeping scripts current. "What's the latest pricing for the Canon R50?" gets a real-time answer, not training data from months ago. For news commentary, product reviews, and trending topic channels, this alone justifies ChatGPT over other script-writing tools.

The custom GPT feature lets you build a script-writing assistant trained on your style. Feed it transcripts from your best-performing videos, specify your cadence preferences (short sentences for emphasis, questions to camera, callback humor), and every future script matches your voice. Our channels each have a custom GPT that writes first drafts requiring minimal editing.

**Pros:**
- Natural conversational tone — scripts sound human when read aloud
- Web browsing keeps facts and pricing current
- Custom GPTs learn and replicate your specific style
- Voice mode lets you dictate ideas while editing or commuting

**Cons:**
- Can be too generic without custom training — invest time building your GPT
- Occasionally pads scripts with filler (watch for "without further ado" and similar phrases)
- GPT-4 required for best quality — free tier output is noticeably weaker

**Pros:**

## Building Your YouTube AI Stack

The tools above cover every stage of production, but you don't need all 12. Start with your bottleneck.

**If you're just starting (0-1K subscribers):**
Claude (free) for research and outlines. Canva (free) for thumbnails. CapCut (free) for editing. Total: $0/month. Focus on making videos, not buying tools.

**If you're growing (1K-100K subscribers):**
Add ElevenLabs ($5/mo) for voiceover or voice consistency. vidIQ ($17/mo) for SEO research. Opus Clip (free tier) for Shorts repurposing. Total: ~$22/month.

**If you're scaling (100K+ subscribers or multi-channel):**
The full stack. Synthesia for presenter content, Midjourney for custom visuals, TubeBuddy for A/B testing, Suno for branded audio. Total: ~$150-200/month. At this level, every tool should save you more time than it costs — if it doesn't, drop it.

The creators who grow fastest in 2026 aren't the ones with the best equipment or the biggest budgets. They're the ones who compress their production pipeline so they can publish more, test more, and learn faster. AI tools are the compression engine.
