## How to Choose: Decision Framework

**For portfolios under 50 units:** Start with RentRedi (screening/collections), Latchel (maintenance), and Rentastic (pricing). Total cost: $250-600/mo. This covers your core pain points without enterprise overhead.

**For portfolios 50-300 units:** Buildium provides the best integrated foundation at $1-3/unit/mo. Layer in Tenant Turner if you're scheduling 10+ showings weekly. Add Rentastic for pricing optimization. Total cost: $150-700/mo depending on showing volume.

**For portfolios 300-1,000 units:** AppFolio becomes cost-effective at $2-4/unit/mo. Supplement with Latchel if maintenance coordination is complex. Rentastic or Entrata's pricing module for revenue optimization. Total cost: $800-3,000/mo.

**For portfolios 1,000+ units:** Entrata's enterprise capabilities ($8-15/unit/mo) justify the cost through advanced reporting, predictive analytics, and compliance automation. All other tools mentioned integrate with Entrata's API. Total cost: $8,000-20,000/mo.

## Key Implementation Tips

1. **Data migration takes longer than expected.** Budget 2-4 weeks for historical tenant, lease, and maintenance data to sync correctly. Rushing creates reporting gaps.

2. **Train your team before launch.** Most adoption failures happen because staff aren't confident with new systems. Allocate 4-6 hours of training per staff member.

3. **Integrate payment processing early.** Late-payment reminders and automated collections only work if your payment system is connected. This is often the bottleneck.

4. **Start with one AI tool, not five.** Pick your biggest pain point (leasing, maintenance, or pricing), implement that AI tool fully, then layer in adjacent tools. This prevents integration chaos.

5. **Set success metrics first.** Define what you're measuring (days-to-fill, no-show rate, payment collection time, rent growth) before implementation. It's much harder to measure retroactively.
