## How to Choose the Right AI Tools

Start by mapping your biggest operational pain point to a solution category. Most contractors face one of four problems:

**Problem: Scheduling takes 2+ hours daily. Solution:** Housecall Pro, Jobber, or ServiceTitan (dispatch optimization is the primary ROI lever).

**Problem: Technicians miss upsells and customer follow-up happens randomly. Solution:** XOi (automatic upsell detection) or CompanyCam (visual documentation that drives conversations).

**Problem: Can't see which jobs actually profit. Solution:** BuildOps (profitability analytics and smart pricing).

**Problem: Need service agreement automation and recurring revenue visibility. Solution:** Successware or ServiceTitan (service agreement scheduling and tracking).

Evaluate candidates across three dimensions:

1. **Operational Scope** — Does the platform handle your core pain points? If you need dispatch optimization and profitability analytics, a single integrated platform (ServiceTitan, FieldEdge) is better than bolting together specialized tools.

2. **Implementation Burden** — Can you launch in 1-2 weeks (Housecall Pro, CompanyCam) or do you need 6-12 weeks of professional setup (ServiceTitan, Successware)? Match the timeline to your capacity.

3. **Total Cost of Ownership** — Calculate annual cost including per-user fees, setup, training, and integrations. A $4,000/month platform that saves $100k in labor and margin is a bargain; a $1,000/month platform that saves nothing is waste.

Before committing, ask vendors: What's the typical ROI timeline? Can we do a 14-day trial with real jobs? How difficult is the export if we want to leave? Who pays for integration setup? How much training does our team need?
