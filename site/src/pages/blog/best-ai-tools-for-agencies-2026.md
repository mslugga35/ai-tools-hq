## 1. Claude — Best for Strategic Briefs, Research & Client Decks

If your agency lives in Google Docs and Figma, Claude is where strategy happens. Not the flashy, tweet-ready responses—the deep thinking that separates strategic agencies from execution-only shops.

Claude handles the work that directly impacts your hourly rate: competitive analysis, brand positioning arguments, campaign strategy, client research summaries, and the kind of structured thinking that usually takes a strategist 3–4 hours and generates a $2,000 deliverable. The biggest advantage over ChatGPT? Claude doesn't hallucinate when analyzing documents. Feed it a 50-page client annual report and it will surface three real insights without making things up.

Real workflow: Your team lands a new D2C e-comm client. Instead of one strategist spending 8 hours researching the market, you dump 10 competitor websites + 3 industry reports into Claude with a prompt like "Analyze these 10 competitors' positioning statements. What 3 positioning angles are *not* being used? For a [client type], what would resonate?" You get back a structured brief with real gaps and angles. Your strategist uses that as a starting point, adds judgment and client history, and has a $4,000 position recommendation in 2 hours instead of 8.

Price-wise, Claude's free tier covers most small agencies. The paid tier ($20/month) removes rate limits and gets you access to better reasoning. For an agency billing $150+ per hour, a $20/month tool paying for itself 10x over is a no-brainer.

**Price:** Free, or $20/month for Pro

**Free Tier:** 200K tokens/month in free tier, covers 3–4 strategy docs per week

**Pros:**
- No hallucinations on document analysis (critical for client research)
- Handles long-form outputs (full campaign decks, positioning docs)
- Integrates into your workflow via web, API, or desktop
- Strong reasoning for strategic decisions, not just surface-level answers

**Cons:**
- Slower response time than ChatGPT (not suitable for real-time client conversations)

Descript transcribes the full episode, converts the transcript into an editable document, and lets you cut sections by literally deleting text. Remove a "um," remove the "um" from the audio. Split sentences, split the video clip. It's the closest thing to text editing for video/audio.

For agencies producing client content (podcasts, YouTube shows, training videos), this cuts editing time by 50–60%.

**Price:** $12–$25/month

**Free Tier:** Limited to 1 hour/month (enough to test)

## How to Actually Implement This (Don't Get Overwhelmed)

**Month 1: Foundation** — Pick Claude + Jasper + Canva. Integrate into two client projects. Measure time saved per deliverable type. Set baseline.

**Month 2: Expansion** — Based on time savings, add one more tool (usually Synthesia or Midjourney depending on your service mix). Run one full campaign with all three tools integrated.

**Month 3+: Optimization** — You now have data. Expand the tools that showed ROI, kill the ones that didn't. Build playbooks and templates for each client type.

The mistake most agencies make: buying all 12 tools at once, overwhelming the team, and having everyone resort to old methods because the new ones are "too complicated." Start small, prove ROI, expand systematically.
