## FAQs

### Which AI video tool is best for beginners?
**InVideo AI** — generous free tier, simple interface, thousands of templates to get started immediately.

### Can AI videos replace human presenters?
For many use cases, yes. Corporate training, product demos, and explainer videos are now routinely made with AI avatars at Fortune 500 companies. High-stakes human connection (sales calls, keynotes) still benefits from real humans.

### Is Synthesia or HeyGen better?
Depends on your use case. **Synthesia** for enterprise/compliance requirements and the largest avatar library. **HeyGen** for marketing personalization and a lower starting price.

### How long does it take to create an AI video?
Most platforms let you go from script to finished video in under 15 minutes. Training videos typically take 30-60 minutes depending on length and editing needs.

### Are AI-generated videos detectable?
Increasingly, yes — detection tools exist. For business use this rarely matters. For impersonation or misinformation, it's unethical regardless of detection.
