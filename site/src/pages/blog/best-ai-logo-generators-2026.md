## Tips for Getting the Best Results

**1. Write specific prompts, not vague ones.**
Bad: "Make me a logo."
Good: "Minimalist geometric logo for a sustainable fashion startup, leaf and circle combined, navy and gold, flat design, no gradients, must work at 1-inch size."

**2. Generate 50+ variations and look for outliers.**
Most AI outputs cluster around similar solutions. Dig through the second and third batches. The best ideas are often the ones that look slightly wrong on first glance.

**3. Test your favorite at small sizes (1-inch favicon).**
A logo that looks great at 2-inches might be illegible as a favicon. Generate small previews before you finalize.

**4. Use the free tiers to explore before you commit.**
Looka, Ideogram, and Brandmark all let you generate unlimited logos for free. Spend 30 minutes exploring before you pay.

**5. Get feedback from people outside your company.**
Show your top three to 5-10 people in your target market. You'll hear patterns about what resonates. Your co-founder loves it? Good. Do your customers?

**6. Don't overthink iterations.**
Three rounds of feedback is the sweet spot. After that, you're optimizing for marginal improvements. Move on and launch.

**7. Combine AI output with light designer polish.**
If you have $200 in the budget, spend $49 on Looka and $150 on a designer to refine the winner. This gives you 80% of the value at 25% of the cost.
