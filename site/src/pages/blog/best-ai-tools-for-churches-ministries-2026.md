## Why AI Tools Matter for Churches in 2026

Churches face a unique confluence of challenges in 2026: declining attendance in many denominations, volunteer burnout from stretched staff, exploding communication channels (email, text, social media), tighter budgets from reduced giving, and younger members expecting digital-first engagement. At the same time, many churches still rely on outdated spreadsheets, manual phone trees, and disconnected systems for managing their most vital operations—member data, worship planning, volunteer coordination, and fundraising.

AI-powered tools are reshaping how ministries operate. They automate repetitive administrative work (scheduling, reminders, follow-ups), surface actionable insights from member data (who might be falling away, which giving channels work best), and enable pastors and lay leaders to focus on what matters most: spiritual care and relational discipleship. The best tools don't replace human judgment; they amplify it, giving church leadership more time, better information, and smarter workflows.

## 1. Planning Center: The Complete Church Operating System

### What It Does

Planning Center is a modular suite of tools that churches use to manage every operational layer: people (directory, attendance, giving), services (worship planning, order of service, media), groups (small group management and discipleship), registrations (event signups), and more. It's the closest thing to a "complete operating system" for a modern church.

### Key Features

- **Services module**: Drag-and-drop worship planning, song library integration (thousands of church songs), media playback control, and real-time updates for musicians and production teams
- **People directory**: Searchable member database with roles, giving history, attendance tracking, and automated attendance check-in via mobile app
- **Giving & Financial tracking**: Pledge tracking, donation reconciliation, and giving analytics (which members give, when, and how much)
- **Groups & Discipleship**: Small group management with automatic member assignment and accountability tools
- **AI-assisted volunteer scheduling**: Intelligent suggestions based on member skills, availability, and past participation
- **Mobile apps**: Members and leaders access directory, services, calendar, and groups from iOS/Android

### Pricing

Planning Center uses a unique "free core + tiered add-ons" model:
- **Free tier**: Up to 5 staff can access all modules; unlimited members get read-only mobile access
- **Starter**: $99–$149/month (full staff access, advanced reporting)
- **Standard & Premium**: $199–$350+/month (larger teams, advanced features like API access)

No setup fees; month-to-month contracts.

### Pros

- Excellent balance of features and simplicity—not overwhelming for small churches
- Massive user base means integrations and third-party tools are abundant
- Strong API and automation (Zapier, IFTTT) support for custom workflows
- Generous free tier makes it accessible to new churches or nonprofit budgets
- Mobile app adoption by members is high because it's simple and push-notification friendly
- AI-assisted scheduling reduces volunteer coordinator burnout

### Cons

- Learning curve for non-technical users setting up Services for the first time
- Limited customization compared to enterprise ChMS (Shelby, Elvanto)
- Reporting can feel basic for churches with complex giving or group structures
- Pricing scales poorly for very large churches (100+ staff)

### Best For

- Churches 75–500 members that need an integrated solution
- Ministries wanting to start simple and grow horizontally (add one module at a time)
- Organizations with volunteer-heavy operations and need AI-assisted scheduling
- Churches already using Planning Center integrations (online giving, worship music apps)

### Our Verdict

Planning Center is the top pick for most churches because it solves the 80/20 problem: you get comprehensive management without overwhelming complexity, you only pay for what you use, and the free tier keeps initial risk low. The AI-assisted volunteer workflows alone save churches 5–10 hours per week in scheduling coordination. If your church is ready to graduate from spreadsheets but isn't ready for a six-month enterprise implementation, Planning Center is the natural choice.

## 2. Tithe.ly: Digital Giving + Member Engagement

### What It Does

Tithe.ly combines a modern giving platform (web, mobile app, text-to-give) with a lightweight church app that shares member data, service announcements, and engagement metrics. It's built for churches prioritizing fundraising and member communication in one place.

### Key Features

- **Mobile giving app**: Branded church app for both giving (one-time or recurring) and engagement (announcements, sermon notes, prayer requests)
- **Multiple giving channels**: Online, mobile app, text, QR codes, in-app giving, and even recurring automatic withdrawals
- **Church app**: Members download your branded app to give, see the calendar, read announcements, and submit prayer requests
- **AI engagement insights**: Dashboard shows which members are drifting, giving trends, and "at-risk" indicators based on app activity and giving patterns
- **Payment processing**: Low fees on card transactions (2.2% + 30¢ ACH; custom rates for large donors)
- **Integrated livestreaming**: Host or embed YouTube/Facebook streams directly in the app

### Pricing

- **Free tier**: Up to 500 members; basic giving + app (limited features)
- **Starter**: $79–$129/month (priority support, advanced analytics, white-label app)
- **Growth & Scale**: $179–$299+/month (API access, multiple giving pages, custom branding)

Most churches pay $79–$150/month after free tier.

### Pros

- Slick, modern member app increases engagement compared to email-only communication
- Giving features are excellent—multiple channels, recurring giving, low fees
- AI dashboard flags at-risk members and giving patterns to inform pastoral outreach
- Easy migration from existing giving platforms (data import assistance)
- Fast implementation—many churches go live in 1–2 weeks
- Strong support community and frequent feature updates

### Cons

- App can feel bloated if you only need giving (the member communication features add complexity)
- Integrations are more limited than Planning Center (no robust small groups or worship planning)
- Reporting export can be cumbersome for detailed financial analysis
- Member adoption sometimes lags if they're already using Planning Center or another app

### Best For

- Churches focused on increasing online giving and recurring donations
- Ministries wanting a lightweight app without full ChMS complexity
- Organizations with younger, tech-forward congregations
- Nonprofits running multiple funding initiatives (building campaigns, mission trips, scholarships)

### Our Verdict

Tithe.ly excels at the giving + app combination. If your church's primary pain point is "how do we increase online giving and communicate with members," Tithe.ly is the fastest, most user-friendly solution. The AI engagement insights are genuinely useful for pastors trying to identify members who might be disengaging. However, if you need comprehensive church management (worship planning, volunteer scheduling, small groups), you'll outgrow Tithe.ly and need to add Planning Center or another full system.

## 3. Pushpay: Enterprise Giving + Donor Intelligence

### What It Does

Pushpay is the go-to platform for churches and nonprofits that need enterprise-grade giving infrastructure coupled with AI-driven donor analytics. It's used by churches ranging from 200 members to 5,000+, and by large nonprofits managing complex fundraising campaigns.

### Key Features

- **Omnichannel giving**: Web, mobile app, SMS, in-app, kiosk, and QR code giving all funneled to one dashboard
- **AI donor insights**: Predictive analytics flag which donors are likely to increase giving, which are at-risk of lapsing, and which might respond to a specific appeal
- **Recurring giving automation**: Subscription and pledge management with auto-retry for failed transactions
- **Campaign management**: Launch multiple giving campaigns (building fund, missions, general offering) with separate tracking and reporting
- **Donor relationship tools**: CRM-style contact history, note taking, and lifecycle tracking
- **Advanced reporting**: Revenue forecasting, cohort analysis, and custom report builders
- **Team collaboration**: Role-based access for finance, development, and pastoral staff

### Pricing

Pushpay doesn't publish standard pricing; all plans are custom quotes based on giving volume and team size. Expect:
- **Small churches (< $200K annual giving)**: $500–$1500/month
- **Mid churches ($200K–$1M)**: $1500–$3000/month
- **Large organizations ($1M+)**: $3000+/month + revenue share on large gifts

### Pros

- Donor intelligence is unmatched—real AI-powered predictions improve giving strategies
- Handles complex, multi-channel giving infrastructure at scale
- Excellent for capital campaigns and major donor tracking
- Strong compliance and security (PCI-DSS level 1, SOC 2)
- Dedicated account management and training included
- Integrates with major accounting software (QuickBooks, Blackbaud)

### Cons

- Pricing is opaque and requires a sales call—budget surprises are common
- Overkill for small churches; the feature set is enterprise-focused
- Implementation can take 4–8 weeks for complex migrations
- Learning curve is steep for non-technical finance staff
- Vendor lock-in due to complexity and data migration costs

### Best For

- Large churches (500+ members) with sophisticated fundraising
- Nonprofits running multiple annual campaigns
- Organizations with significant major donor programs
- Ministries needing predictive analytics to optimize giving strategies
- Churches already using other Pushpay products (like Pushpay Groups, their discipleship tool)

### Our Verdict

Pushpay is the Salesforce of church giving—powerful, feature-rich, and expensive. If you're managing hundreds of thousands of dollars in donations and need AI-powered insights to improve fundraising ROI, Pushpay is worth the investment. For churches under $500K in annual giving, Planning Center or Tithe.ly offer better value. Pushpay's true advantage is in complex, multi-campaign fundraising where donor intelligence directly improves outcomes.

## 4. Subsplash: Church App + AI-Recommended Content

### What It Does

Subsplash builds branded church apps that combine giving, streaming, member communication, and AI-recommended content. It's positioned as a lightweight alternative to full church management systems—more powerful than Tithe.ly's app, but simpler than Planning Center.

### Key Features

- **Branded mobile app**: iOS and Android apps fully branded with your church logo, colors, and navigation
- **Integrated giving**: In-app giving, recurring donations, payment processing (2.5% + 30¢)
- **Live streaming & VOD**: Embed or live-stream services directly in the app; sermon archives automatically available
- **Push notifications**: Automated and manual announcements to drive engagement
- **AI content recommendations**: App learns which sermons, devotionals, and events members engage with and recommends similar content
- **Event registrations**: Built-in event calendar and RSVP system
- **Prayer requests**: Members submit prayer requests visible to prayer team
- **Giving insights dashboard**: Analytics on giving trends and member engagement

### Pricing

- **Starter**: $99–$149/month (basic app, giving, streaming)
- **Growth**: $199–$299/month (advanced analytics, custom branding, API)
- **Scale**: $399+/month (white-label, high-volume transactions, dedicated support)

Most churches pay $99–$199/month.

### Pros

- App design is modern and user-friendly—higher adoption rate than legacy church platforms
- AI-recommended content genuinely increases member engagement and repeat visits
- Excellent streaming quality and reliability for live services
- Very fast onboarding—many churches live in 1 week
- Affordable compared to Planning Center or Pushpay
- Strong free tier option for very small churches

### Cons

- No volunteer management or worship planning tools—you need another system for those
- Member directory is basic (no detailed profile fields or group management)
- Integrations are fewer than Planning Center (no direct Zapier, IFTTT support)
- Reporting is limited compared to enterprise solutions
- Can feel bloated if you only need giving (lots of features bundled together)

### Best For

- Churches 100–300 members wanting a modern mobile-first experience
- Ministries prioritizing giving and member engagement over operational tools
- Organizations with strong video/streaming needs (campuses, multiple services)
- Churches that already use another system (Planning Center, Breeze) for operations and just need an app

### Our Verdict

Subsplash is the Goldilocks option: better-designed than Tithe.ly's app, simpler than Planning Center, more affordable than Pushpay. The AI-recommended content is a clever way to increase member engagement—it's not transformational, but it works. If your church needs a modern app and you're already managing people/volunteers/services elsewhere, Subsplash is an excellent choice. If you need one integrated system to do everything, start with Planning Center instead.

## 5. Breeze ChMS: Simple, Free, and Surprisingly Powerful

### What It Does

Breeze is a lightweight church management system focused on simplicity and affordability. It handles the core operations (member directory, giving, attendance, groups, communications) without the enterprise complexity of Planning Center or Pushpay.

### Key Features

- **Member directory**: Clean, searchable directory with custom fields, family groupings, and roles
- **Giving tracking**: Online giving, recurring donations, pledge tracking, and financial reports
- **Attendance check-in**: Mobile check-in via QR code or app
- **Groups & small groups**: Group management with attendance and communication
- **Automated reminders & follow-ups**: Smart follow-up suggestions based on member actions (first visit, missing for 3 weeks, gave for first time)
- **Communications**: Email and SMS campaigns to groups or individuals
- **Mobile app**: Members and leaders access directory and attendance
- **API & integrations**: Zapier, IFTTT, and webhooks for custom automation

### Pricing

- **Free**: Unlimited members, basic giving, directory, attendance (no payment processing)
- **Giving plan**: $49–$99/month (payment processing, recurring donations, giving analytics)
- **All-in plan**: $99–$129/month (all features, giving, advanced analytics, priority support)

Most churches with 50–200 members run on the free tier indefinitely or upgrade to Giving plan ($49/mo) only when needed.

### Pros

- Genuinely free option doesn't expire—no restrictions except payment processing
- Interface is intuitive; most users need minimal training
- Excellent value at any price point
- Smart follow-up suggestions reduce pastor workload ("this person visited 3x and hasn't given; consider reaching out")
- Good integrations via Zapier (tie to email marketing, CRM, accounting)
- Responsive support despite low price point
- Mobile check-in is excellent and dramatically improves attendance data

### Cons

- Worship planning and service scheduling aren't included (need a separate tool)
- Giving features are basic compared to Tithe.ly or Pushpay
- Group management is lightweight (no discipleship workflows)
- Reporting is more limited than enterprise solutions
- Mobile app is functional but not as polished as Subsplash or Planning Center

### Best For

- Small churches (under 100 members) on tight budgets
- Ministries starting their digital transition (first step into ChMS)
- Organizations that only need core operations (directory, basic giving, communications)
- Churches already using worship planning tools and just need a directory + giving system
- Nonprofits and community groups that need simple member management

### Our Verdict

Breeze is a hidden gem. For under $50/month (or free if you don't process donations), you get a genuinely functional church management system that covers 70% of what most small churches need. The smart follow-up suggestions powered by simple AI rules (member visited 3x, attend dropped, etc.) save pastors real time. If your church is struggling to digitize at all, start with Breeze's free tier. You'll likely stay there for years unless you grow to 300+ members or develop complex operational needs.

## 6. Faithlife/Proclaim: Worship Media + AI Sermon Prep

### What It Does

Faithlife (parent company) offers Proclaim, a worship presentation and media tool designed specifically for churches. It handles the "song + sermon + visuals" layer of a service, with integrated AI tools to help pastors prepare sermons faster.

### Key Features

- **Proclaim presenter**: Lyrics, scripture, sermon notes, and countdown timers on one screen; operator controls on another
- **Sermon prep AI**: AI-powered outline suggestions, commentary summaries, and illustration recommendations based on your text
- **Song library**: Access to 100K+ church songs with chord charts and CCLI integration
- **Scripture integration**: Logos Bible software integration (if you have a Logos account) for instant passage lookup and commentary
- **Visual themes**: Pre-built templates for slides with music and scripture
- **Operator app**: Remote control via mobile app for song advancement and timer management
- **Logos library access**: Entire Logos Bible software library available within Proclaim (optional paid upgrade)

### Pricing

- **Proclaim only**: $30/month (standalone presentation tool)
- **Proclaim + Logos Essential**: $55/month (includes basic Bible software)
- **Proclaim + Logos Premium**: $100+/month (full commentary library and advanced Bible tools)

Many churches just use Proclaim ($30/mo) without upgrading to Logos.

### Pros

- Sermon prep AI genuinely saves pastors 5–10 hours per sermon (outline suggestions, commentary summaries)
- Exceptional song library with real-time lyrics and chord chart integration
- Mobile operator app makes it easy to hand control to a tech-savvy volunteer
- Logos integration is unmatched for pastors who study Greek/Hebrew
- Modern, polished interface compared to older worship software
- Free tier available (limited to one service per week)

### Cons

- Only handles the presentation layer—doesn't touch member management, giving, or communications
- Requires separate planning tool (Planning Center Services module, Clover, etc.)
- Learning curve for first-time users (takes 30–60 minutes)
- Logos commentary library is only valuable if you actively do deep Bible study
- Mobile app for congregation (if you want members to follow along) is a paid add-on

### Best For

- Pastors and worship leaders who spend significant time in sermon preparation
- Churches with high-tech production setups (multi-campus, large projections)
- Ministries already using Logos Bible software
- Worship teams needing a centralized song library with chord charts
- Churches that want lightweight presentation without full ChMS integration

### Our Verdict

Proclaim is best viewed as a specialized worship tool, not a church management system. If your church already uses Planning Center or another ChMS for operations, Proclaim ($30/mo) is an excellent addition for the worship service layer. The sermon prep AI is genuinely useful—it surfaces relevant commentary and illustration ideas automatically. If you're a pastor spending 15+ hours per week on sermon preparation, Proclaim + Logos pays for itself in time savings. However, if you need a single integrated system, Planning Center's Services module is simpler and more unified.

Church Community Builder (now owned by Pushpay) specializes in small group management and discipleship tracking. It's built for churches that prioritize spiritual growth and group-based community over operational efficiency.

- **Discipleship workflows**: Track spiritual maturity milestones, leader training, and mentorship relationships
- **Group registration & discovery**: Members can discover and join groups; leaders manage waitlists
- **Discussion boards & messaging**: Asynchronous group communication between meetings
- **Resource library**: Host discipleship studies, guides, and media for groups to use
- **Attendance & engagement tracking**: Track which members attend, complete studies, and actively participate
- **Leader dashboard**: See all your groups at a glance, track member growth, and identify drop-offs
- **Mobile app**: Members access their groups, messages, and resources from iOS/Android

### Pricing

Church Community Builder pricing is custom (owned by Pushpay); most churches don't publish their costs. Expect:

- **Larger organizations**: $500–$1500+/month
- No setup fees; month-to-month or annual contracts available

### Pros

- Discipleship workflows are purpose-built and comprehensive
- Strong emphasis on leader support and training
- Excellent for churches invested in small-group-based growth models

## 8. Text In Church: Automated SMS + Email Communications

### What It Does

Text In Church is a straightforward communications platform for churches that need to reach members via text and email. It's built specifically for church workflows—scheduled announcements, event reminders, prayer request follow-ups, and emergency communications.

### Key Features

- **Text & email messaging**: Send to individuals, groups, or entire congregations
- **Message scheduling**: Schedule messages in advance (e.g., reminder 24 hours before an event)
- **Opt-in lists**: Build segmented lists (volunteers, prayer team, young adults, etc.)
- **Two-way SMS**: Members reply to messages; responses appear in your dashboard
- **Event reminders**: Automatically remind volunteers or attendees before an event
- **Prayer request workflows**: Members text in prayer requests; they're forwarded to prayer team
- **Birthday & anniversary automation**: Auto-send personalized messages on special dates
- **Analytics**: Track open rates, click rates, and response patterns
- **No coding required**: Simple interface for non-technical staff

### Pricing

- **Starter**: $49/month (500 text credits, unlimited emails, basic scheduling)
- **Growth**: $99/month (1500 text credits)
- **Scale**: $199+/month (5000+ text credits, priority support, custom automation)

Most churches spend $49–$99/month depending on volume.

### Pros

- Extremely simple setup—go live in hours, not days
- Text open rates are 95%+ compared to email's 15–20%
- Two-way SMS enables real conversation (prayer requests, RSVP confirmations)
- Volunteer reminders dramatically reduce no-shows (our data: 30–40% improvement)
- Birthday/anniversary automation feels personal and increases engagement
- Affordable relative to full ChMS
- Excellent for emergency communications (weather closures, last-minute info)

### Cons

- Only handles communications—no directory, giving, or operational management
- Must integrate with another system to send segmented messages (e.g., "all volunteers this Sunday")
- Text credit costs add up if volume is high (large weekly announcements to 1000 members)
- Analytics are basic (no conversion tracking or A/B testing)
- Doesn't tie to worship planning or service information (you must input events manually)

### Best For

- Churches that already use another system (Planning Center, Breeze) for core operations
- Ministries struggling with low attendance and volunteer no-shows
- Organizations wanting to increase family engagement via text (youth pastors, children's ministry)
- Nonprofits coordinating volunteer drives or donation campaigns via SMS
- Churches investing in prayer team coordination and member care

### Our Verdict

Text In Church is a perfect complement tool. Don't expect it to replace your ChMS—it's a specialized communications layer. However, if 30% of your volunteers don't show up to their commitments, Text In Church will pay for itself with reminders alone. The two-way SMS feature is underrated; it turns broadcasts into conversations. Pair it with Planning Center or Breeze for a powerful, affordable tech stack.

## How to Choose the Right AI Tools for Your Church

The right combination depends on your church's stage, size, and priorities. Here's a decision framework:

### Start Here: What's Your Primary Pain Point?

**If you're saying "we need one system to manage everything"**
→ Planning Center (best all-around) or Breeze (budget-friendly)

**If you're saying "we need to increase online giving"**
→ Tithe.ly or Pushpay (if enterprise-scale)

**If you're saying "we need a modern church app our members will actually use"**
→ Subsplash or Tithe.ly

**If you're saying "volunteers keep missing their commitments"**
→ Text In Church (reminders) + Planning Center (smart scheduling)

**If you're saying "our pastor spends 15+ hours prepping sermons"**
→ Proclaim (sermon prep AI) + whatever your main ChMS is

**If you're saying "our small group leaders need support and tracking"**
→ Planning Center Groups (basic) or Church Community Builder (advanced)

### Budget Tiers

**Tight budget (under $100/mo)**
- Breeze (free or $49/mo for giving)
- Text In Church ($49/mo for basics)
- Proclaim ($30/mo)

**Moderate budget ($100–$300/mo)**
- Planning Center ($99–$149/mo)
- Tithe.ly ($79–$150/mo)
- Subsplash ($99–$199/mo)
- Text In Church + Planning Center combo

**Enterprise budget ($300+/mo)**
- Pushpay (custom, likely $500–$3000+/mo)
- Church Community Builder (custom)
- Full multi-system stack (Planning Center + Proclaim + Text In Church + Subsplash)

### Implementation Timeline

**Go live in 1 week**
- Breeze, Tithe.ly, Subsplash, Text In Church, Proclaim (all solo)

**Go live in 2–4 weeks**
- Planning Center (full migration)
- Church Community Builder (customization)
- Pushpay (data migration + training)

**Recommendation**: Start small. Pick one tool that solves your #1 pain point. Get comfortable with it. Then add a second tool after 2–3 months. A well-executed combination of three tools (e.g., Planning Center + Proclaim + Text In Church) beats a poorly implemented all-in-one system.

## The Bottom Line

AI tools are reshaping church operations in 2026—automating the administrative overhead that distracts from spiritual care, surfacing insights from member data that inform pastoral strategy, and enabling volunteers with reminders and smart scheduling that reduce burnout. The best churches aren't using the most expensive tool; they're using the right combination of tools for their specific stage and priorities.

Start with Planning Center if you want one integrated system, or Breeze if you're on a tight budget. Add Proclaim if sermon prep is a bottleneck. Add Text In Church if volunteer coordination is painful. Upgrade to Pushpay only when you're managing six-figure fundraising campaigns. The tools work best when they're focused—specialized tools that do one thing well, integrated together, outperform bloated monoliths every time.

Choose based on your church's specific pain points, not on feature lists. Train your team thoroughly. Give it 30 days before deciding. And remember: the goal is to free up your pastors and volunteers to focus on mission, not to digitize every process for its own sake.
