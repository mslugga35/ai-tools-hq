## Quick Summary

Looking for the fastest AI tool? **Planner 5D** wins for realistic 3D walkthroughs and floor plans. Need mood boards and inspiration fast? **Collov AI**. Want precision photo-realistic renders? **Midjourney**. Read on for detailed breakdowns of all 8 tools, real pricing, and which one fits your workflow.

---

## 1. Interior AI

**Price:** Free / Pro $9.99/month / Business $19.99/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, 3 renders/month

**Free Trial:** Yes, 5 free renders on signup

Interior AI is the most accessible entry point for designers exploring AI visualization. Upload a room photo, describe your design vision, and get multiple render options in seconds. The free tier gives you 3 renders monthly—enough to test the tool's core strength: converting existing spaces into polished interior designs.

**Pros:**
- Fastest renders (15-30 seconds)
- Clean, minimal interface (low learning curve)
- Works from single photos (no need for 3D models)
- Renders show furniture placement and lighting instantly
- Good for quick client mood boards

**Cons:**
- Limited architectural detail in renders
- Can't edit floor plans or structural elements
- Pro plan only adds 15 renders/month (not unlimited)
- Doesn't generate furniture layouts, only visual styles
- No collaboration features

**Best for:** Solo designers who want fast mood boards, client presentations, before/after comparisons, and quick style explorations.

---

## 2. Planner 5D

**Price:** Free / Plus $4.99/month / Professional $9.99/month / Business $19.99/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, full 2D/3D access with watermark

**Free Trial:** Yes, 7-day professional trial

Planner 5D is the Swiss Army knife for interior designers. It combines floor planning, 3D rendering, furniture catalogs, and material libraries in one platform. The free version is genuinely powerful—you get 2D and 3D modes, thousands of furniture pieces, and the ability to create complete room plans. Professionals upgrade for high-res renders and collaboration.

**Pros:**
- Floor plan + 3D rendering in one tool
- Massive furniture/material library (70K+ items)
- Photorealistic walk-through mode
- Export floor plans, 2D drawings, 3D renders
- Collaboration tools on paid plans
- Mobile app for on-site measurements
- Best-in-class for residential design

**Cons:**
- Interface can feel dense (steeper learning curve)
- Free version adds watermark to renders
- AI design suggestions limited (mostly manual placement)
- Material textures sometimes look plastic
- Professional plan ($9.99/mo) still limits high-res exports

**Best for:** Residential designers, small studios, designers who need floor plans + 3D renders, clients who want to see their space before committing.

---

## 3. Collov AI

**Price:** Free / Pro $12/month / Team $30/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, limited designs with Collov branding

**Free Trial:** Yes, 3 AI generations free

Collov AI is the style-matching engine. Upload a room photo and describe your aesthetic (minimalist, eclectic, bohemian, etc.), and it generates mood boards with furniture recommendations. Unlike pure rendering tools, Collov focuses on *curation*—finding actual furniture pieces from real retailers and suggesting color palettes. Designers love it for narrowing down style direction before detailed planning.

**Pros:**
- Best mood board generator (mood + furniture pairing)
- Recommends furniture from real retailers (shoppable)
- Fast iteration on style concepts
- Good for interior trends research
- Reasonable pricing
- Team collaboration on higher tiers

**Cons:**
- Doesn't generate floor plans or 2D drawings
- No 3D walk-through mode
- Furniture recommendations skew Western market
- Limited customization of recommendations
- Exports are boards, not technical drawings

**Best for:** Designers doing style consultation, mood board creation, furniture sourcing, trend research, client conversations about aesthetic direction.

---

## 4. HomeDesigns AI

**Price:** Free / Creator $9.99/month / Studio $24.99/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, 1 design/month with watermark

**Free Trial:** Yes, 3 free AI generations

HomeDesigns AI is the newcomer gaining traction with small studios. It combines photo-to-design rendering with floor plan generation and material specifications. Where it differs: you get dimensional accuracy and material cost estimates—useful when pitching projects to builders and contractors.

**Pros:**
- Generates material specs and cost estimates
- Floor plan + 3D render in one AI prompt
- Good dimensional accuracy for real construction
- Faster learning curve than Planner 5D
- Creator plan ($9.99/mo) is reasonable
- Good for presenting to contractors/builders

**Cons:**
- Newer platform (smaller furniture catalog)
- Material estimates sometimes off market prices
- AI occasionally misinterprets room dimensions
- Collaboration features limited
- Exports less flexible than Planner 5D

**Best for:** Designers working with contractors, renovation specialists, budget-conscious studios, anyone who needs material specs with renders.

## 5. REimagineHome

**Price:** Free / Starter $9/month / Professional $24/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, 2 AI designs/month with watermark

**Free Trial:** Yes, 2 free credits on signup

REimagineHome positions itself as "Instagram for interior design"—a social platform *plus* AI rendering. While the social component isn't core to most designers' workflows, the rendering engine is strong. It's best used for inspiration hunting and client mood boarding.

**Pros:**
- Great for trend research (community designs)
- Fast rendering (photo to redesign in 30 seconds)
- Clean, modern UI
- Good for generating multiple style options quickly
- Affordable starter plan
- Social discovery of design trends

**Cons:**
- No floor planning tools
- Social features can be distracting
- Limited export options (mostly web-ready images)
- Smaller furniture database than Planner 5D
- Collaboration requires sharing public links

**Best for:** Designers seeking inspiration, trend research, quick client mood boards, solo practitioners who like social design communities.

## 6. Spacely AI

**Price:** Free / Pro $15/month / Studio $35/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, 1 render/week free

**Free Trial:** Yes, 2 free renders on signup

Spacely AI specializes in accurate spatial reasoning. Upload a room photo and describe changes, and it generates renders with better understanding of how furniture actually *fits* in the space. It's less about style and more about feasibility—crucial when redesigning tight apartments or odd-shaped rooms.

**Pros:**
- Best spatial accuracy for tight layouts
- Understands furniture scale and placement constraints
- Good for small space solutions
- Renders show practical furniture arrangements
- Reasonable pricing
- Fast iteration on layout options

**Cons:**
- Not focused on aesthetic direction (less stylish output)
- Limited furniture customization
- Smaller design community than Planner 5D
- No social/inspiration features
- Can't export floor plans directly

**Best for:** Small space specialists, apartment designers, spatial problem-solving, furniture arrangement validation.

---

## 7. Midjourney

**Price:** Basic $10/month / Standard $30/month / Pro $120/month (billed annually)

**Free Plan:** No, but 25 free trial messages

**Free Trial:** Yes, 25 free prompts for testing

Midjourney is the photo-realistic generalist. While not purpose-built for interior design, designers use it extensively for high-end renderings, mood boards, and concept ideation. The upside: it produces gallery-quality images that impress clients. The downside: you're writing prompts, not clicking furniture pieces.

**Pros:**
- Photorealistic quality (best for client wow-factor)
- Versatile across design styles (modern, bohemian, luxury, etc.)
- Unlimited generations on Standard/Pro plans
- Best for mood boards and concept art
- Community inspiration and shared prompts
- Exports high-resolution images

**Cons:**
- Steep learning curve (prompt engineering required)
- No floor plans or architectural accuracy
- Can't edit specific furniture pieces
- Requires Discord workflow (not ideal for some teams)
- More expensive than single-task design tools
- Inconsistent furniture accuracy across variations

**Best for:** Designers focused on aesthetics over spatial accuracy, high-end residential projects, concept presentations, design teams that can leverage shared prompts.

---

## 8. RoomGPT

**Price:** Free / Pro $9.99/month

**Free Plan:** Yes, unlimited designs with watermark

**Free Trial:** Yes, immediate free access

RoomGPT is the minimalist's choice. Upload a photo, write a simple text description ("modern minimalist bedroom"), and get a redesign. No furniture libraries to navigate, no floor plans—just photo-to-AI-render. The free plan is genuinely unlimited (with watermark).

**Pros:**
- Simplest interface (upload + describe)
- Unlimited free renders (with watermark)
- Fast processing (30-45 seconds)
- Best for quick iterations
- Lowest barrier to entry
- Good for client-facing quick mood boards

**Cons:**
- No floor plans or spatial accuracy
- Can't control specific furniture pieces
- Watermark on free version (distraction for clients)
- Limited customization of output
- No material specs or cost estimates
- Smallest team (least development activity)

**Best for:** Individual designers exploring AI, quick client presentations, fast mood board generation, designers on a budget.

## Which AI Interior Design Tool Should You Choose?

**For Quick Client Presentations:** Start with **Interior AI** or **RoomGPT**. Both render fast and require minimal input. Interior AI is slightly more polished; RoomGPT is cheaper.

**For Complete Projects:** Use **Planner 5D**. It's the only tool that handles floor plans, 3D renders, furniture placement, and material specs in one workflow. Ideal for residential designers delivering comprehensive plans.

**For Furniture Sourcing + Style:** Choose **Collov AI**. It's unmatched at pairing moods with actual furniture pieces and retailers. Perfect for style consultants and e-design specialists.

**For High-End Concept Work:** Pick **Midjourney**. It generates the most impressive, gallery-quality mood boards. Best for luxury residential and pitching premium projects.

**For Small Space Problem-Solving:** Go with **Spacely AI**. It understands spatial constraints better than other tools. Essential if you specialize in apartments or tight layouts.

**For Budget-Conscious Solo Designers:** Use **RoomGPT** for daily rendering and **Interior AI** for client presentations. Combined, they cost ~$10/month and handle 90% of typical workflows.

**For Construction-Ready Deliverables:** Use **HomeDesigns AI**. It's the only AI tool that generates material specs and cost estimates alongside renderings—critical when working with contractors.

## Final Thought

Interior design is entering an era where concept-to-presentation happens in minutes, not days. These eight tools each solve different problems: speed, accuracy, style, collaboration, spatial reasoning, or cost estimation. The best choice depends on whether you're optimizing for client delight (Midjourney), spatial precision (Planner 5D), sourcing efficiency (Collov AI), or speed (Interior AI/RoomGPT).

Start with free trials on 2–3 tools that match your primary workflow need. Most designers end up with a two-tool combo: one for quick rendering, one for detailed planning. Give yourself a week to test; the tool that saves you the most time is the right one.
