## What Synthesia Actually Does

Synthesia is an AI video generation platform that lets you create videos with AI avatars (virtual presenters) and voiceovers, no camera or actor required. You write a script, pick an avatar and voice, and the platform generates a finished video in minutes. It's not deepfake technology — Synthesia uses prerecorded avatars that animate based on your text, which keeps quality consistent and makes the process straightforward.

The workflow is simple: write or paste your script, pick your avatar (there are 100+ options, ranging from diverse professional personas to industry-specific characters), choose a voice or upload your own audio, select a template background, and generate. Export takes 5-20 minutes depending on video length.

In 2026, Synthesia's avatar quality has noticeably improved. The lip sync is tighter, the eye contact feels more natural, and the gesture variety is less robotic than it was two years ago. It's not Hollywood-level, but it's professional enough for internal communication and B2B content.

## Where Synthesia Falls Short

### Avatars Still Look AI-Generated
In close inspection, the tells are obvious. Lip sync breaks on complex consonants. Eye movement is limited. Gestures repeat. If your content is going to be scrutinized frame-by-frame (a pitch deck, an executive address), the uncanny valley shows. For 90% of viewers on first watch, it's fine. For high-stakes contexts, it's not.

### Limited Gesture and Movement
Avatars can't walk, turn around dramatically, or move across the frame. You're watching a talking head. It works for explainers and training, but if you want dynamic video, you'll find the limitation frustrating.

### Script-Dependent Quality
Bad scripts produce bad videos, but Synthesia doesn't help with scripting. If you're not a writer, you'll get mediocre output. A teleprompter voice reads your script with no emotion or emphasis — you have to write performance into the script itself, which is harder than it sounds.

### No Real-Time Customization
You generate, wait for render, notice the pace is off, regenerate. There's no editing timeline where you can trim pauses or adjust timing. You either re-script or accept what you got.

### Avatar Customization Costs Extra
If you want your CEO's likeness as an avatar (instead of a generic persona), budget $500-2,000 and 1-2 weeks for a custom avatar training session.
