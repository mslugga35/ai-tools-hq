## Video Creation

### 1. Synthesia — Best for Faceless Video Production

**Price:** $29/month (Starter) | $89/month (Creator) | Custom (Enterprise)

Synthesia turns a script into a professional video with an AI avatar — no camera, no lighting, no editing. Pick from 230+ realistic avatars, paste your script, and get a polished video in minutes. It's the go-to for course creators, corporate trainers, and anyone who needs video without being on camera.

**Pros:**
- 230+ AI avatars with natural lip sync and gestures
- 140+ languages with native-sounding voiceover
- Built-in screen recorder and slide import
- Brand kits for consistent styling across videos
- Collaboration features for teams

**Cons:**
- Avatars still feel slightly "uncanny valley" in close-ups
- Limited creative control vs. traditional video editors
- Monthly video minute limits on lower plans

**Best for:** Course creators, corporate training, product demos, explainer videos

[Try Synthesia →](https://synthesia.io/pricing?via=matt-morales&sub1=content)

### 2. Opus Clip — Best for Short-Form Repurposing

**Price:** Free tier | $19/month (Starter) | $49/month (Pro)

Opus Clip takes a long video (YouTube, Zoom, podcast) and automatically clips it into viral-ready shorts. It identifies the most engaging moments using an AI "virality score," adds captions, and formats for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. For creators sitting on hours of long-form content, this is free money.

**Pros:**
- AI virality scoring picks the best moments
- Auto-captions with animated word highlighting
- One-click formatting for TikTok, Reels, Shorts
- B-roll suggestions and filler word removal
- Batch processing for multiple videos

**Cons:**
- Virality score isn't always accurate for niche content
- Caption styling options are limited on free tier
- Requires decent source video quality

**Best for:** YouTubers, podcasters, anyone repurposing long-form to short-form

## Audio & Voice

### 4. ElevenLabs — Best Overall Voice AI

**Price:** Free tier (10k chars/mo) | $5/month (Starter) | $22/month (Creator) | $99/month (Pro)

ElevenLabs is the gold standard for AI voice. Clone your voice from a 1-minute sample, generate voiceovers in 29 languages, or design a completely new voice by describing it. The quality is indistinguishable from professional voice actors for most use cases.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class voice naturalness and emotion
- Voice cloning from as little as 1 minute of audio
- 120+ pre-built voices across 29 languages
- Voice design — describe a voice, it creates it
- Projects feature for long-form narration with multiple speakers
- Sound effects and music generation

**Cons:**
- Costs add up with heavy usage
- Voice cloning quality depends on input audio
- Free tier is limited to 10,000 characters/month

**Best for:** Podcasters, YouTubers, audiobook producers, game developers

### 5. Descript — Best for Podcast & Video Editing

**Price:** Free tier | $33/month (Pro) | $40/month (Business)

Descript treats audio/video like a Google Doc — edit the transcript and the media follows. Delete a sentence from the text, and it's cut from the video. It also has "Overdub" voice cloning for fixing mistakes without re-recording.

**Pros:**
- Transcript-based editing is incredibly intuitive
- Overdub lets you fix mistakes by typing corrections
- Automatic filler word and silence removal
- Built-in screen recording and teleprompter
- Direct publishing to YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts

**Cons:**
- Transcription accuracy drops with heavy accents
- Overdub voice needs training data
- Pro features require $33/month commitment

**Best for:** Podcasters, video editors, anyone who works faster with text than timelines

- **Output quality** — Does it produce work you'd actually publish?

- **Integration** — Does it play well with your existing stack?
