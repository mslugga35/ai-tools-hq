# Best AI Tools for Wedding Planners & Event Planners in 2026

Wedding planning is exhilarating—client calls, vendor coordination, timeline management, design approvals. It's also exhausting. Between crafting personalized emails to 30 vendors, designing mood boards, managing timelines across multiple events, and keeping clients updated, even experienced planners find themselves working 60+ hour weeks.

This is where AI tools step in. In 2026, the right combination of AI platforms can cut administrative time by 15-20 hours per event while improving client communication and creative output.

I've tested eight AI tools that wedding and event planners are actually using—not hypothetically, but in production, across real events. Here's what works, what doesn't, and how to integrate these tools into your planning business.

**Real Workflow Example**

David, an events coordinator for corporate galas and milestone celebrations, uses ChatGPT for timeline and logistics planning. He prompts: "Create a 6-month timeline for a 500-person gala with outdoor ceremony, indoor reception, live band, and catering for 3 dietary restrictions. Key vendor deadlines, guest communication timeline, and day-of schedule."

Within 30 seconds, ChatGPT generates a detailed 12-month timeline with vendor deadlines, invitations schedule, food tastings, final confirmations, and a minute-by-minute day-of rundown. David reviews and customizes (changes specific dates, adds his vendor contacts), then shares it with the client and team.

For vendor communication, David uses ChatGPT to draft emails. Instead of staring at a blank screen for 20 minutes, he describes the situation: "Write a follow-up email to the florist about the timeline change from March to April, maintaining a professional but friendly tone."

ChatGPT delivers a polished draft in 20 seconds. David edits for personal touches and sends.

**Real Numbers**

- Timeline generation: 30 seconds (vs. 45 minutes manual creation)
- Vendor email drafting: 2 minutes including edits (vs. 15-20 minutes)
- Script/announcement writing: 3 minutes (vs. 30 minutes brainstorming)
- Ideas/brainstorming sessions: Unlimited (vs. paying for consultants)
- Monthly cost: $20 (ChatGPT Plus) or free with limitations

**Pricing**

- Free: Basic ChatGPT, limited usage
- ChatGPT Plus: $20/month (unlimited usage, faster responses, access to advanced models)
- ChatGPT Pro: $200/month (more advanced reasoning, vision analysis)

**Pros**

- Versatile—solves 10+ planning tasks without additional tools
- Fast—most responses in under 60 seconds
- Cost-effective—one subscription replaces multiple specialized tools

- Excellent at copywriting and tone matching

**Cons**
