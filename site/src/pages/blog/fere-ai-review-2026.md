## What Fere AI Does

Fere AI operates as an always-on agent across three core functions:

**1. Portfolio Intelligence**
Connects to exchanges (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, and more) and monitors wallet addresses directly on-chain. Aggregates your positions across Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, and other major chains into a single view. Tracks PnL, cost basis, unrealized gains, and DeFi positions — including LP tokens, lending positions, and staked assets.

**2. Market Signals & Alerts**
This is where Fere AI earns its "AI" label. Rather than simple price alerts, Fere's agent monitors sentiment signals, on-chain flows, whale wallet movements, funding rates, and technical indicators simultaneously. When a signal triggers, you get a reasoned alert — not just "ETH is down 5%" but context about why, what's driving it, and what similar setups have historically resolved to.

**3. Autonomous Execution**
For users who opt in: Fere AI can act on its own signals within parameters you define. Example configurations:
- "If ETH funding rate exceeds 0.1% for 6 consecutive hours, reduce my perpetuals position by 20%"
- "If my stablecoin yield drops below 8% APY, move to the highest-yielding protocol on my approved list"
- "Alert me immediately if any wallet I monitor moves more than 5 ETH in a single transaction"

Execution is permissioned — you define limits, approve strategies, and can pause at any time. Fere doesn't have unconstrained access to your funds.

## What We'd Improve

**Onboarding is complex for non-technical users.** Connecting wallets and exchanges, setting up execution permissions, and configuring signal thresholds takes time. Not difficult, but not plug-and-play either. If you're new to DeFi or non-custodial wallets, expect a learning curve.

**No stock/equity integration.** Fere is crypto-only. If you want one tool for your entire investment portfolio (stocks + crypto), you'll need a separate solution for the TradFi side.

**Signal backtesting is limited.** You can see historical signal accuracy in aggregate, but can't easily backtest a specific strategy configuration against historical data before going live.

**Pricing tiers can feel restrictive at lower tiers.** Some features we found most valuable (autonomous execution, advanced multi-chain tracking) require the higher-tier plans.
